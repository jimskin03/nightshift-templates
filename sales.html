Straight answers

Before you request a preview.

What does “72 hours” mean?

It is the pilot target for a publish-ready first version after we receive usable copy, contact details, and assets. Delays in content, approvals, or third-party services can move the launch date.

Is hosting included?

Launch guidance is included. Ongoing hosting and maintenance can be quoted separately so you can compare it with hosting the supplied files yourself.

Can I use my existing domain?

Yes. We can guide the domain connection after approval. Domain registration or renewal fees remain yours.

Will you guarantee Google rankings or new leads?

No. We include sound metadata, structure, and performance foundations, but rankings and leads depend on competition, reputation, content, and marketing outside the website.

What do you need from me?

Your business name, services, service areas, preferred contact route, opening hours, licence or trust details you can substantiate, and the best photos available.
